About

Hi! I'm a 2nd-year CS student at Purdue, passionate about building faster and more efficient computing systems. I'm especially interested in the intersection of software engineering and chip design, where new architectures can unlock the next generation of AI performance.

Through hands-on internships at both semiconductor design (Purdue) and AI systems research (NSF-funded YC startup), I've seen firsthand how future AI workloads will demand breakthroughs in hardware efficiency. This past summer, I teamed with four ECE students to design and tape out a RISC-V CPU graphing-calculator SoC, implementing SystemVerilog and writing RISC-V assembly.

Seeing a real chip we custom-built that could solve user problems made me want to keep pushing deeper into this field.

As AI is limited by computing capacity - I'm motivated by one goal: to engineer innovative computing systems that powers the next era of AI.

My passions:

  • Development of AI-based industry applications
