About

Hi! I'm a 3rd-year CS student at Purdue. Lately I've been building the infrastructure that AI models actually run on. My latest project is a router that keeps coding agent traffic on a self-hosted model, and only reaches out to a frontier model when you opt in, so your code never leaves the network unless you want it to.

I came at this from the hardware side. Last year I teamed with four ECE students to design and tape out a RISC-V CPU graphing-calculator SoC, implementing SystemVerilog and writing RISC-V assembly. Seeing a real chip we custom-built that could solve user problems made me want to keep pushing, just further up the stack.

This past summer I worked at Lenovo as an AI Development Intern, where I built a knowledge-management RAG prototype and a B2B price-optimization model.

AI is limited by computing capacity, so I'm motivated by one goal: to engineer innovative computing systems that power the next era of AI.

My passions:

  • Development of AI-based industry applications
  • Chip design and verification
